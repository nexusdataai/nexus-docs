**Totogi CallSense** capitalizes on this market gap by delivering **AI-powered call analytics** that:

- Provides **100% automated QA coverage** (vs. 30% manual)
- Reduces manager QA time by **50%**
- Delivers **25-55x ROI** with multi-million dollar revenue impact

## What It Is

**Totogi CallSense** is an enterprise-grade AI-powered call processing and analytics platform that automatically processes, transcribes, and analyzes sales calls to drive revenue growth and operational efficiency.

### Core Capabilities:

- **🎙️ AI Transcription & Diarization** - Automatic speech-to-text with speaker identification
- **🧠 Real-time AI Analysis** - Sentiment, compliance, product mentions, objections
- **📊 Automated Quality Assurance** - 100% call coverage with AI-powered scoring
- **💡 Coaching Recommendations** - Data-driven insights for rep improvement
- **📈 Performance Analytics** - Conversion rates, adherence, quality trends
- **🤖 Product Recommendations** - AI-suggested upsells and cross-sells
- **📝 Automatic Quote Generation** - Transform conversations into accurate quotes

### Deployment via Totogi Toolkit:

Totogi CallSense is deployed to customer AWS infrastructure through **Totogi Toolkit** - a self-service deployment platform that provisions complete infrastructure (frontend, backend, databases, integrations) in **under 30 minutes** with **zero professional services costs**.
