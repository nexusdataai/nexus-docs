# NexusAI

## An Intelligent AI Operating Layer for Enterprise Systems

> Transforming Enterprise Data into Actionable, Reasoning AI

## Overview

NexusAI transforms enterprise data into **reasoning AI that actively runs and optimises business operations**. Instead of passive insights, NexusAI enables intelligent systems that **understand, decide, and execute**.

## Platform Layers

The NexusAI Enterprise Reasoning Platform is organised into four functional layers:

- **Solutions Layer** — packaged AI-native applications and ID services delivered to business users.
- **Reasoning Plane** — AI Copilot, AI Agents, ReAct Tools, and Policy Guardrails for governed multi-step reasoning.
- **Control Plane** — semantic ontology, knowledge graph, and policy bundle that capture the structure of the business.
- **Data Plane** — uniform virtual data access across structured, unstructured, streaming, graph, vector, and event data.
