    Architecture Pattern¶

    Every application in this catalogue follows a consistent execution pattern:

    flowchart LR
    A["Ontology Layer<br/><i>Resolved Entities</i>"] --> B["AI Workflow Engine<br/><i>LLM + Clinical Rules</i>"]
    B --> C["Dashboard & Alerts<br/><i>Real-time Visualization</i>"]
    B --> D["Action Layer<br/><i>Recommendations · Orders · Escalations</i>"]
    1. Data Ingestion — Ontology layer resolves entities across EHR, Revenue Cycle, HIS, and unstructured clinical data (patient matching, encounter linking, terminology normalization)
    2. AI Workflow Execution — LLM-powered pipelines apply clinical logic, predictive models, coding intelligence, and operational optimization
    3. Presentation — Dashboards surface clinical KPIs, financial metrics, and operational status; alerts trigger on clinical deterioration, denial patterns, and capacity constraints
    4. Action — Recommendations surface in clinician workflows (EHR alerts), revenue cycle queues (denial follow-up), and operational dashboards (bed management, OR scheduling)
